=== 1. Standard identity (public abstract, ASTME903-20) ===
- "Standard Test Method for Solar Absorptance, Reflectance, and Transmittance
  of Materials Using Integrating Spheres."
- Scope highlights:
  1.1  Covers spectral absorptance/reflectance/transmittance measurable with
       spectrophotometers equipped with integrating spheres.
  1.2  Includes methods of computing solar-weighted properties from the
       measured spectra.
  1.3  Applicable to materials with both specular and diffuse optical
       properties.
  1.7  Solar-weighted results shall be calculated with the standard solar
       spectral distributions referenced by the standard; results with other
       spectra may be reported if the spectrum is stated.
- Significance (Sec 5): designed for solar energy system material
  characterisation; reproducibility is a goal; practical for smooth
  materials with specular and diffuse reflection; the relevant spectral
  range is much wider than the photopic (visual) response.
- Solar absorptance from the measured data: alpha_solar = 1 - rho_solar - tau
  (conservation of energy at each wavelength), so rho_solar is simply the
  solar-weighted reflectance term used in the glare pipeline.

=== 2. Solar weighting procedure (per SolarPACES V3.1 Sec 4.4 / Sec 7.2) ===
Weighting uses the currently valid standard solar spectrum for direct
irradiance, ASTM G173 (AM 1.5 for mid-latitudes). Equations follow
weighted-ordinate / integration form:

    rho_solar([La,Lb], theta) = int(rho_lam(lam,theta) * S(lam) dlam, La..Lb)
                                / int(S(lam) dlam, La..Lb)

where S(lam) is the reference solar irradiance (W/(m^2*nm)) and La..Lb the
wavelength window the weighting is computed over.
- G173 publishes solar irr from 280-4000 nm.
- The far-NIR contribution 2500-4000 nm is only ~0.0018 of the total and the
  UV tail below 320 nm ~0.0018 -> SolarPACES recommends computing solar
  weighting over [320, 2500] nm (convenient symmetric ~0.36% cut).
- Recommended spectral step <= 10 nm (5 nm nominal).
- rho_lam must be the SPECTRAL reflectance measured at the same incidence
  angle theta (near-specular / hemispherical; see 3).

=== 3. Hemispherical vs specular reflectance (SolarPACES V3.1 Sec 4) ===
- rho_lam,h(lam,theta): hemispherical reflectance = TOTAL reflected into the
  upper hemisphere (specular + diffuse), measured with an integrating sphere.
- rho_lam,phi(lam,theta,phi): near-specular reflectance within a half-accept-
  ance cone phi (CSP/glare relevant). For a perfectly smooth surface the
  distribution peaks in the specular direction; the glare hazard uses the
  specular beam, not the diffuse scatter.
- Practical consequence for glare: for SMOOTH glass (float/low-iron) the
  diffuse component is negligible, so hemispherical ~= specular; for
  textured glass the hemispherical number OVERestimates the glare beam. The
  code's E903-style mode therefore models the specular front-surface
  component (Fresnel) and reports the hemispherical/specular caveat.

=== 4. Mapping to the code implementation (hazard.py::solar_weighted_reflectivity) ===
- Panel ρ(lam,theta) has no published measured spectrum -> E903-20-style
  procedure is implemented with a physical spectral model:
  * unpolarised Fresnel reflectance of the front air|glass interface at AOI
    theta using a representative PV-glass refractive index n(lam) (dispersion,
    ~1.52 in the visible -> ~1.46 at 2500 nm);
  * a single front surface is justified because the solar cell absorbs the
    transmitted light, so the module's external reflectance ~ first-surface.
  * optional quarter-wave ARC stack (air|ARC|glass) via `n_arc` to emulate
    anti-reflection coated glass (SGHAT "smooth_arc" counterpart).
- Solar weighting uses the ASTM G173 direct-normal spectrum already present
  in the repo (pvlib.spectrum.spectrl2 -> iec62471.py::_spectrum()), the
  direct-normal (beam) component being the correct weight for glare.
- Integration window: [320, 2500] nm per SolarPACES recommendation (the
  pvlib G173 output covers [280,4000]; the code clips to 320-2500).
- Result R_solar(theta) is the "solar-weighted reflectance" of E903-20's
  philosophy, applied per glare minute at that minute's AOI, replacing the
  broadband SGHAT v6 Table 1 fit.

=== 5. Expected differences vs SGHAT v6 Table 1 (to document in report) ===
- Origin:  SGHAT fits = empirical AOI fits measured on real PV glass samples
           (Yellowhair & Ho 2015); E903-style mode = physical Fresnel model
           of bare/ARC glass, solar-weighted spectrally.
- Normal:  SGHAT smooth 4.41% / smooth_arc 3.24%; Fresnel n~1.52 ~4.0%
           (bare) / ~3.2% with practical ARC -> comparable.
- Mid-AOI: SGHAT polynomial minimum ~40 deg; Fresnel Brewster dip ~atan(n)
           ~56-57 deg -> curves diverge in 40-65 deg band.
- Grazing: SGHAT exponential + clip R=0.08 past 89 deg; Fresnel rises to
           ~0.7 at 89 deg (saturated, no 0.08 clip) -> E903-style mode gives
           HIGHER reflectance at the glare AOIs (78-82 deg) by ~1.5-1.8x,
           i.e. proportionally higher retinal irradiance (classification
           unchanged, hit detection unchanged).
- ARC:     real ARC also suppresses glancing reflectance; bare-Fresnel is the
           conservative (higher-R) bound for coated modules.
